How to get a key
  1. Open GitHub → new fine-grained token.
  2. Under Repository access, choose Only select repositories → learn-thai.
  3. Under Add permissions, search Contents and set it to Read and write.
  4. Generate it and paste it below.

How it works
  1. Review (like Anki): phrases that are due come back. Say each one out loud, then rate it: Again, OK, Good or Easy.
  2. Lesson (like Pimsleur): first hear a short conversation between Niwat and Premwadee. Then learn its phrases, with a few old ones mixed in. Then role-play your side of the conversation. Finally, hear it once more.

Auto marks everything OK and keeps going, for listening hands-free. Next ▸ skips ahead, I know this skips a phrase for a week. In Words, Make next lesson chooses which lesson comes next.
