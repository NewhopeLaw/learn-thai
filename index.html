How it works
  1. Listen. You hear a new Thai phrase. Long ones are built up from the end, a piece at a time.
  2. Recall out loud. The narrator asks, “How do you say…?” Say the Thai out loud during the pause, before you hear the answer.
  3. Check and repeat. You hear the right answer twice. Repeat it, copying the tones.
  4. Rate yourself: Again, Good or Easy. Phrases you find hard come back sooner.

Within a session, phrases come back at growing intervals. Across days, a spaced-repetition schedule brings each one back just before you'd forget it. Try one session a day.
