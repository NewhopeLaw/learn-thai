      Back up to GitHub

      Saves your progress to the learn-thai repo after every session, so it's backed up and shared between your devices. Set this up once per device:

      1. Open GitHub → new access key (a "fine-grained token").
      2. Pick the longest expiration. Under Repository access, choose Only select repositories → learn-thai.
      3. Under Permissions → Repository permissions, set Contents to Read and write.
      4. Generate it and paste it here. This browser remembers it from then on.

      How it works
      1. Listen. You hear a new Thai phrase. Long ones are built up from the end, a piece at a time.
      2. Recall out loud. The narrator asks, “How do you say…?” Say the Thai out loud during the pause, before you hear the answer.
      3. Check and repeat. You hear the right answer twice. Repeat it, copying the tones.
      4. Rate yourself: Again, Good or Easy. Phrases you find hard come back sooner.

      Good or Easy finishes a phrase for today. Again brings it back later in the session until you get it. Across days, a spaced-repetition schedule brings each one back just before you'd forget it. Try one session a day.

      Already know a phrase? Press I know this to skip it for a week, or use I know this unit under Your progress. During a quiz you can press Good as soon as you know the answer, without waiting for the audio.

      Keyboard: 1 Again · 2 Good · 3 Easy · K I know this · V switch voice · R replay · Space pause · Esc stop
