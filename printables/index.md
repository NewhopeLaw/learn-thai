# Printable worksheets <span class="thai">แบบฝึกหัด</span>

Free handwriting and vocabulary worksheets to print, one for each unit of the [audio course](../). Each is a 6-page PDF on US Letter paper (it prints fine on A4 too).

**What's on each worksheet**

- **Page 1:** two key sentences from the unit, broken down word by word with romanization and meaning, plus two short grammar notes.
- **Pages 2–4:** 9 words from the unit. Trace the gray copies on Thai guide lines, then write each word yourself. Colored tags show the tone of every syllable, and a small blue label under each letter shows the sound it makes.
- **Pages 5–6:** the unit's sentences and two bonus phrases. Read the first row aloud, trace the second, and write the third yourself.

The sentences use the male forms ผม and ครับ. Women say ฉัน instead of ผม, and ค่ะ (statements) or คะ (questions) instead of ครับ.

**Tips for using them**

- Most Thai letters start at their small loop. Begin there, then follow the line.
- Write the vowels that sit above or below a letter, and the tone marks, last.
- Say each word out loud as you write it. Writing and speaking together helps it stick.
