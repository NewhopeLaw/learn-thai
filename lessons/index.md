# Stage 2: Reading and writing <span class="thai">เรียนภาษาไทย</span>

**Start with the [audio course](../) first.** Like a child learning to speak, get comfortable hearing and saying Thai before you read it. A good time to start these lessons is when you can comfortably say most of Units 1–3 from memory.

Once your ear knows the sounds, the Thai script makes much more sense, because the spelling tells you the tone.

| # | Lesson | You will learn |
|---|--------|----------------|
| 1 | [How Thai works](01-how-thai-works) | The writing system, romanization, and the 5 tones |
| 2 | [Consonants](02-consonants) | All 44 consonants and their three classes |
| 3 | [Vowels](03-vowels) | Short and long vowels and where they sit around a consonant |
| 4 | [Tone rules](04-tone-rules) | Working out a syllable's tone from its spelling |
| 5 | [Greetings & survival phrases](05-phrases) | The audio course phrases, now in writing |
| 6 | [Numbers](06-numbers) | Counting, Thai digits, prices |
