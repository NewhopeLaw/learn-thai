# Lesson 4: Tone rules

Thai spelling tells you the tone exactly. You need three facts about a syllable:

1. The **class** of the first consonant (middle / high / low — Lesson 2)
2. Whether the syllable is **live** or **dead**
3. Which **tone mark**, if any, is written above it

## Live vs. dead syllables

- **Live** syllables end in a **long vowel** or in **m, n, ng, -i, -w**.
  e.g. <span class="thai">มา</span> maa, <span class="thai">กิน</span> kin
- **Dead** syllables end in a **short vowel** or in **-k, -t, -p**.
  e.g. <span class="thai">จะ</span> jà, <span class="thai">ปาก</span> pàak

## The tone marks

| Mark | Name |
|------|------|
| <span class="thai">่</span> | <span class="thai">ไม้เอก</span> mái èek |
| <span class="thai">้</span> | <span class="thai">ไม้โท</span> mái thoo |
| <span class="thai">๊</span> | <span class="thai">ไม้ตรี</span> mái trii (middle class only) |
| <span class="thai">๋</span> | <span class="thai">ไม้จัตวา</span> mái jàt-ta-waa (middle class only) |

## The tone chart

| Class | Live, no mark | Dead, no mark | <span class="thai">่</span> | <span class="thai">้</span> | <span class="thai">๊</span> | <span class="thai">๋</span> |
|-------|---------------|---------------|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| **Middle** | mid | low | low | falling | high | rising |
| **High** | rising | low | low | falling | — | — |
| **Low** | mid | short vowel: **high**<br>long vowel: **falling** | falling | high | — | — |

Notice that **low-class consonants "shift up"**: the same mark gives a tone one step higher than for the other classes. That's why <span class="thai">ไม่</span> (low class + <span class="thai">่</span>) is *falling*, but <span class="thai">ไก่</span> (middle class + <span class="thai">่</span>) is *low*.

## Worked examples

| Word | Class | Live/dead | Mark | Tone | Meaning |
|------|-------|-----------|------|------|---------|
| <span class="thai">ปา</span> paa | middle | live | — | mid | throw |
| <span class="thai">ป่า</span> pàa | middle | live | ่ | low | forest |
| <span class="thai">ป้า</span> pâa | middle | live | ้ | falling | aunt |
| <span class="thai">ขา</span> khǎa | high | live | — | rising | leg |
| <span class="thai">ข้าว</span> khâao | high | live | ้ | falling | rice |
| <span class="thai">คา</span> khaa | low | live | — | mid | stuck |
| <span class="thai">ค่า</span> khâa | low | live | ่ | falling | value |
| <span class="thai">ค้า</span> kháa | low | live | ้ | high | trade |
| <span class="thai">รัก</span> rák | low | dead, short | — | high | love |
| <span class="thai">มาก</span> mâak | low | dead, long | — | falling | very / much |

## Leading ห (silent h)

A silent <span class="thai">ห</span> in front of a low-class sonorant (<span class="thai">ง ญ น ม ย ร ล ว</span>) makes it behave like **high class**:

<span class="thai">มา</span> maa (come, mid) → <span class="thai">หมา</span> mǎa (dog, rising)

## Exercise

Work out the tone:
<span class="thai">ไก่ · ไข่ · ห้า · ดี · พ่อ · สวย</span>

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answers</summary>

- <span class="thai">ไก่</span> kài — middle + ่ → **low** (chicken)
- <span class="thai">ไข่</span> khài — high + ่ → **low** (egg)
- <span class="thai">ห้า</span> hâa — high + ้ → **falling** (five)
- <span class="thai">ดี</span> dii — middle, live → **mid** (good)
- <span class="thai">พ่อ</span> phɔ̂ɔ — low + ่ → **falling** (father)
- <span class="thai">สวย</span> sǔai — high, live → **rising** (beautiful)
</details>
