# Lesson 1: How Thai works

## The big picture

- Thai has its own alphabet: **44 consonants**, around **32 vowel forms**, and **4 tone marks**.
- It is written **left to right**, usually **without spaces between words**. Spaces mark the end of a phrase or sentence.
- Vowels can appear **before, after, above, or below** the consonant they belong to.
- Thai is **tonal**: the same syllable said with a different pitch is a different word.
- Grammar is friendly: no verb conjugation, no plurals, no articles (*a*, *the*).

## Romanization used in this course

Romanization is only a bridge to the Thai script. This course uses:

| Symbol | Sounds like | Example |
|--------|-------------|---------|
| `k`, `t`, `p` | unaspirated — like the *k* in "s**k**ip" (no puff of air) | <span class="thai">ไก่</span> kài (chicken) |
| `kh`, `th`, `ph` | aspirated — like English *k*, *t*, *p* (with a puff). **`ph` is never "f"**, **`th` is never "th" in "the"** | <span class="thai">ไข่</span> khài (egg) |
| `j` | between *j* and *ch*, unaspirated | <span class="thai">จาน</span> jaan (plate) |
| `ng` | *ng* in "si**ng**", can start a word | <span class="thai">งู</span> nguu (snake) |
| doubled vowel `aa`, `ii`, `uu`… | long vowel | <span class="thai">มา</span> maa (come) |
| `ue` | like *oo* with spread lips (no English equivalent) | <span class="thai">มือ</span> muue (hand) |
| `ɔ` | *aw* in "l**aw**" | <span class="thai">พ่อ</span> phɔ̂ɔ (father) |
| `ɛ` | *a* in "c**a**t" | <span class="thai">แม่</span> mɛ̂ɛ (mother) |
| `oe` | like *u* in "f**u**r" | <span class="thai">เธอ</span> thoe (you/she) |

## The 5 tones

Tone marks in the romanization show the pitch:

| Tone | Mark | Pitch | Example | Meaning |
|------|------|-------|---------|---------|
| Mid | (none) | flat, normal voice | <span class="thai">มา</span> maa | come |
| Low | à | flat, lower | <span class="thai">ใหม่</span> mài | new |
| Falling | â | high, then drops | <span class="thai">ไม่</span> mâi | not |
| High | á | high, slightly rising | <span class="thai">ม้า</span> máa | horse |
| Rising | ǎ | dips low, then rises | <span class="thai">หมา</span> mǎa | dog |

> **Practice sentence:** <span class="thai">ไม้ใหม่ไม่ไหม้ไหม</span>
> *mái mài mâi mâi mǎi* — "Doesn't new wood burn?"
> Four nearly identical syllables, four different tones.

## Exercise

Say each word five times, exaggerating the tone, then try them in a random order:

<span class="thai">มา · หมา · ม้า · ไม่ · ใหม่</span>
